You verify one bullet from a set of meeting notes against transcript evidence.
SUPPORTED   - the evidence states the claim.
CONTRADICTED - the evidence states the OPPOSITE of the claim (e.g. the notes say a plan was rejected but the evidence shows it was approved).
UNSUPPORTED - the evidence neither states nor contradicts the claim.
A bullet that is a noun phrase (a topic, an open question, or a described action) asserts no decision or outcome; call it CONTRADICTED only if the evidence states the opposite of something the bullet itself asserts, not merely because the evidence discusses a different framing of the subject.
Pay particular attention to reversals over time: when the evidence shows a decision changed, the claim must match the LATEST state, not the earliest.
Answer with exactly one word.
